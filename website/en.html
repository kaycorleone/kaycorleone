Who we are

The Primarschule Affoltern am Albis (primary school) has three school units, each with its own parent council, called Elternrat. Each Elternrat is made up of delegates from the classes. They are elected between the summer and the autumn holidays, usually at the parents' evening.

On this shared website the three parent councils present what they do. We encourage exchange between parents and the school, organise events and parent education, support projects and welcome new families.

Questions about staff, teaching, the curriculum, teaching materials, the timetable and the allocation of children to classes are not part of parent participation under § 55 of the Zurich Elementary School Act (Volksschulgesetz). For these, please contact the class teacher or the school management.

New at school?

Welcome to Affoltern am Albis! Starting at a new school raises many questions. Here you will find the most important answers and people who are glad to help.

Another language? You can read this website in your own language. In Chrome and Edge, right-click on the page and choose «Translate». In Safari on the iPhone, tap «Aa» in the address bar and then «Translate Website».

The first steps

  1. Get to know the class teacher. The class teacher (Klassenlehrperson) is your first contact for all questions about your child.
  2. Go to the parents' evening. There you will learn how the school year works and meet other parents.
  3. Subscribe to the newsletter. That way you will not miss any parent council events.
  4. Find a buddy family. A family who has lived here longer answers your questions and comes along to your first event.

Small glossary

You will see these German words in letters from the school.

Klassenlehrperson (class teacher)
The teacher responsible for the class. The first contact for parents.
Schulleitung (school management)
Leads a school unit. The next contact if a question cannot be settled with the class teacher.
Elternabend (parents' evening)
An information evening held by the class teacher for the parents of a class, usually at the beginning of the school year.
Elternrat (parent council)
Parents who organise events and projects together with the school and bring in the concerns of parents.
Znüni (morning snack)
A small snack for the morning break, for example fruit, vegetables or bread.
Schulergänzende Betreuung (childcare)
Care before and after lessons and at lunchtime. The school administration can give you details.

More terms and rules are explained by the school in the ABC of the Primarschule Affoltern am Albis (in German).

How it works

A short guide to this website and to the ways you can get involved.

  1. Stay informed

    Subscribe to our newsletter at the bottom of every page. A few times per school year you will receive an email with events, projects and calls for help. You can unsubscribe at any time with one click.

    Go to the newsletter

  2. Parent education

    Under «Parent education» you will find our next talks and offers from the region. Use the form to tell us which topics interest you. We plan the next events based on your answers.

    Go to parent education

  3. Help with a project

    Under «Projects» you can see where we need support right now, for example: «Sports day, 6 people from 8 am to 12 noon». Choose the project in the form and send it. We will get back to you with all the details.

    Go to projects

  4. Sign up as a helper

    Under «Help out» you tell us what we may ask you for and how often. We will only write to you when something suits you. Saying no is always fine.

    Go to help out

  5. Share an idea or a concern

    Send ideas for the school under «Projects». The parent council of your school unit discusses every idea at its meeting and gets back to you. Please discuss questions about your child, about teachers or about lessons with the class teacher or the school management.

  6. Join the parent council

    Each school unit has its own parent council. Delegates are elected between the summer and the autumn holidays, usually at your class's parents' evening. If you could imagine taking part, tick «Interest in the parent council» under «Help out». We will contact you before the parents' evening.

  7. What happens to my details?

    The forms send your details by email to the shared address of the parent councils. We pass them on to the parent council of your school unit, use them only for the purpose stated and delete them at any time on request. More in the legal notice and privacy section.

Privacy

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising services and does not embed content from Facebook or other social networks. Links to other websites open their pages; their privacy policies apply there.

When you use a form, your details are sent by email to the shared address of the parent councils. We use them only for the purpose you state in the form, pass them on to the parent council of your school unit and do not share them with third parties. We delete details about tasks and events at the latest at the end of the school year, unless you explicitly wish to stay on our list of helpers.

For the newsletter we store your email address and the school units you have chosen. You can unsubscribe at any time.

You can ask at any time what details we hold about you and request that they be corrected or deleted. Please write to .
